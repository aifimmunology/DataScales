TF-IDF + LSI for ATAC) is the PCR reference and is listed for comparison.


## Findings

### RNA · quality
**PCA + Harmony keeps as much biology as scVI or more, and removes much more batch effect.** scib bio conservation is higher for PCA in every selection of both datasets (rna13m 0.56–0.68 vs scVI 0.50–0.61; mmrna 0.50–0.60 vs 0.47–0.58, coarse and fine labels), and batch correction is higher everywhere (0.53–0.68 vs 0.41–0.54).
Local cell-type purity is split: scVI is ahead on mmrna (0.69 vs 0.63, all cells), PCA is ahead inside rna13m's selections (B cells 0.86 vs 0.81). Retraining scVI on the selection does not beat slicing the stored latent.

### RNA · time
**The stored scVI latent is the only fast option at scale.** Re-clustering 1.2M B cells from rna13m takes 8 s from the latent, 10 min with PCA + Harmony and 9 min retraining scVI; all 13.8M cells take 4 min, 15 min and 33 min.
PCA's cost follows the store, not the selection: rna13m isn't sorted by cell type, so a B-cell selection still reads nearly every chunk. On the sorted mmrna store the same selection takes 21 s.

### ATAC
**PeakVI beats uncorrected LSI on biology at every selection** (cell-type purity +0.03–0.12, fine types up to +0.15; graph connectivity and isolated labels higher), with similar batch correction.
Slicing the stored PeakVI latent matches retraining on bio and takes 1–18 s vs 2–14 min. Retraining only helps batch removal on small B-cell selections (mmatac 0.64 vs 0.43). Run 2 has no LSI + Harmony arm.

### For the datavis pipeline
**RNA:** keep PCA + Harmony as the default; a stored scVI latent is worth adding only as a fast mode for big selections, and per-selection scVI training is not worth its cost. **ATAC:** train PeakVI once per store and slice its latent; skip per-selection training. Sorting stores by cell type would cut PCA selection time on large stores.
Caveats: scib's PCR comparison is 0 for most ATAC runs (not informative there), and ATAC labels likely came from an LSI-style clustering, which flatters the uncorrected LSI on KMeans ARI/NMI.
